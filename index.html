1. Experimental vs. Descriptive

Experimental designs manipulate independent variables to measure cause-and-effect. Descriptive designs observe, document, and categorize patterns without variable manipulation.

Key Distinction: Control Groups & Causality (Experimental) vs. Natural Context & Correlation (Descriptive).

2. Instrument Triangulation

Ensuring validity and reliability by cross-verifying qualitative findings (interviews, observations) with quantitative instruments (surveys, diagnostic tests).

Key Test: Do instruments measure the intended research construct consistently?

3. Ethical Safeguards

Rigorous protection of participant rights through informed consent/assent, data anonymization, risk minimization, and transparent reporting.

Key Principles: Autonomy, Beneficence, Anonymity, Non-Maleficence.
